The Story

In late 2024, Phoenix father Josh Strange lost three dogs to cancer in the span of a few months while his wife was pregnant. He was recording vet visits just to process them later, and nothing existed that kept his pets' history in one place or made sense of it.

So he built VetGPT: an AI pet health assistant that knows your specific pet. Upload years of vet records and the AI knows every diagnosis, medication, and lab value. Ask about a symptom and it recalls the vet visit from three months ago you forgot. Record a vet appointment and get a plain-English summary of everything the vet said.

He even built a memorial into the app (the "Rainbow Bridge" In Memory section) because of his own losses. Every log, every vet visit, every photo is preserved when a pet passes.

VetGPT launched in early 2025 and is now live on iOS and Android, supporting 64+ species and used in 31 countries. AP News covered the launch.

What Makes VetGPT Different

"Every pet app tracks health data. Only VetGPT turns that data into an AI that knows your specific pet."

  • + AI health assistant that accesses your pet's full medical history, medications, lab values, and vet visit notes when answering questions
  • + Document scanner: upload vet records, prescriptions, or lab results and the AI extracts diagnoses, medications, lab values, and follow-ups automatically
  • + Vet visit audio recording with AI transcription: never forget what the vet said
  • + Weekly AI photo comparison: take one photo a week and the AI tracks changes in coat, weight, posture, and eyes
  • + 64+ species with species-aware AI: different health advice for a bearded dragon than a golden retriever
  • + Health score (0-100) that adapts scoring by species, with category breakdowns
  • + Share complete health records with any vet in one tap (PDF or secure link)
  • + Family sharing so everyone in the household stays informed
  • + Vet-complementary: "We don't replace your vet. We make you the most prepared person in the exam room."

Species Supported

Dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters, ferrets, hedgehogs, sugar gliders, parrots, cockatiels, finches, canaries, chickens, ducks, bearded dragons, ball pythons, leopard geckos, turtles, tortoises, chameleons, frogs, axolotls, goldfish, bettas, tropical fish, koi, horses, goats, sheep, alpacas, miniature pigs, capybaras, tarantulas, hermit crabs, and many more.

Species-specific features include water chemistry tracking for fish, shedding cycle logs for reptiles, egg production tracking for poultry, husbandry logging for exotics, and molt cycle tracking for invertebrates.

About the Founder

Josh Strange is a Phoenix-based technologist and pet owner. After losing three dogs to cancer in 2024, he built VetGPT as a solo developer using React Native, Supabase, Claude AI, and OpenAI Whisper. He lives in Phoenix with his wife, son, and two dachshunds (Cruz and Mosley).
